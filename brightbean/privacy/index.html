Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

BrightBean (“the app”) is developed by Raghav (“I”, “me”). It is made for children aged 5 to 9.

Summary

The app collects nothing. It works fully offline, with no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Nothing your child does in the app is sent anywhere, to me or to anyone else.

Information collected

None. The app has no user accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It does not connect to the internet; the Android app does not even request internet access.

Information stored on your device

Everything stays on your device, in the app’s own private storage:

  • Players: a nickname, a picture chosen from the app’s own avatars, and an optional school year. No real names, dates of birth, photos or contact details are asked for.
  • Progress: answers and scores, stars, stickers, worksheet and crossword scores, and the times-table grid.
  • Pictures: colouring and drawings your child saves inside the app. They are not saved to your photo library.
  • Settings: sounds, read-aloud and letter spacing.

I cannot see any of this. It is deleted when you delete the player (Grown-ups → Players) or delete the app. On Android the app is left out of device backups. On iPhone and iPad, your own iCloud device backup (if you use one) may include the app’s data, as it does for any app; that backup belongs to you and I never receive it.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, notifications or any other device feature.

Read-aloud

Questions can be read aloud using your device’s own built-in voice.

Grown-ups area

Settings, the progress report and any link out of the app sit behind a grown-ups check that a young child is unlikely to pass.

Purchases

BrightBean is currently free, with nothing to buy. If that changes, purchases will go through Apple’s App Store or Google Play, behind the grown-ups check, and I will still receive no personal data.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it is designed to meet the UK ICO Age Appropriate Design Code (Children’s Code), Apple’s Kids Category rules and Google Play’s Families policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “Last updated” date, and the app will be updated to match.
