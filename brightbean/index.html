BrightBean

Maths & Colouring for ages 5–9

BrightBean is a calm learning app for children in Reception to Year 4. Maths World has number bonds, adding and taking away, times tables with pictures that explain every answer, practice for the Year 4 Multiplication Tables Check, worksheets and number crosswords. Stars earned there unlock pages in the Colouring Studio.

It works fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking and no data collection.
