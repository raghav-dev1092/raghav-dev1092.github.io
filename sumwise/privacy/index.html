Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Sumwise (“the app”) is developed by Raghav (“I”, “me”).

Summary

The app works entirely on your device. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

Information collected

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your display preferences (currency and theme) on your device so they are remembered between sessions. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

The figures you enter are used only to perform calculations on your device and are not saved or transmitted.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, photos, microphone, files, notifications or any other device feature.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
