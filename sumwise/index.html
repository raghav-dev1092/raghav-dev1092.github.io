Sumwise

EMI & SIP Calculator

Sumwise is a calculator for loans and investments that works completely offline: EMI and repayment schedule, loan comparison, SIP with step-up and inflation, lumpsum, SWP and SIP comparison.

No account, no ads, no tracking and no permissions. Your numbers never leave your phone.
